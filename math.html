Grown-up: how this works

Nine topics with three visibly numbered levels, each with three rounds. Levels are independent of age; all are available. Level 1 uses visible matching, level 2 uses parts and changing rules, and level 3 adds hidden parts, two simultaneous rules and multi-step decisions. Picture actions lead to quantities and number parts; physical objects remain important. Pictures and dot layouts change on a new round, then stay still while counting. Feedback follows your action with no submit button. Level 2's train middle round is open exploration: keep moving passengers to discover different splits. Next becomes available after a move. Completion marks mean played, not mastered. Browser-local saving is optional; offline reopening is not promised.
