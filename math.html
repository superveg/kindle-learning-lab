Grown-up: levels and learning

3: small quantities, one-to-one matching, basic shapes and alternating patterns. 4: quantities to 6, comparison, object arithmetic, sorting by a changing rule. 5: quantities to 10, differences, number parts, shape properties, repeating units and equal-unit measurement. Tap-based moving avoids requiring drag support. There is no timer or speed score. Demonstrate each picture instruction once; narrate in your home language. For full experiences, also use real objects away from the screen.
