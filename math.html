Grown-up: how this works

Twelve topics with up to six visibly numbered levels. Tens Workshop starts at level 4, Share Fairly at level 5, and Fraction Kitchen at level 6. Rough age references are 3–8, but these are task levels, not age gates. Each level offers ongoing random practice; there is no three-question limit. Levels are independent of age; all are available. Level 1 uses visible matching, level 2 uses parts and changing rules, and level 3 adds hidden parts, two simultaneous rules and multi-step decisions. Levels 4–6 add bridging through ten, place value, grouping, fractions and multi-condition reasoning. Picture actions lead to quantities and number parts; physical objects remain important. Task types are shuffled within each level. Quantities, pictures and dot layouts vary on each new question, then stay still while answering. The large arrow keeps practising at the same level; the optional next-level staircase changes level. Back leaves practice at any time. Feedback follows your action with no submit button. Level 2's train split question is open exploration: keep moving passengers to discover different splits. Next becomes available after a move. Completion marks mean played, not mastered. Browser-local saving is optional; offline reopening is not promised.
