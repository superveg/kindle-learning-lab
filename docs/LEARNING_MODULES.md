## Current levels 6–12 quality and subject identity

The owner requested the same depth/quality review used for mathematics across the other five subjects. The earlier upper release reused narrow templates: language mainly tracked possession/transfers; science mainly calculated record differences or repeated one scope answer; stories mainly copied quantities or ordered objects. Reusing an interaction engine is appropriate; relabeling one task as different curriculum is not.

The 90 upper families now contain 1,080 rewritten seeds, within the existing total of 180 families and 2,332 authored seeds. Level 6 remains the established foundation. Numbers identify task structures, not ages, school grades, mastery or measured uniform difficulty.

Each level has three complementary families:

- Logic: transformations with intermediate pictures; deduction from explicit feature clues/useful questions; dependency planning that accepts every legal order. Small/large, shape and fill conditions are real stated predicates. An implication is not its reverse. Quarter-turns, mirrors and before/after arrows have visible keys.
- Chinese and English: language in context; connected passage comprehension; claim/evidence decisions. Twelve original contexts include a notice, a route, a plant observation, a recipe, a rule correction and an observation that revises a prediction. Chinese context vocabulary is separately authored (such as 轻/轻轻, 深, 开, 干, 画 and 好), rather than translated English homonym labels. English covers contextual senses such as light, bat, bank and fall. Shared comprehension skills do not make this a full Chinese literacy or English phonics course.
- Science: interpretation of supplied investigations; planning a useful test; observation/evidence/limits. Records identify the measured quantity, unit, setup and held-alike conditions. Water/ice/cloth-loss records decrease rather than depicting unexplained increases. Repeated records may overlap; an unusual result merits checking. Two controlled comparisons that distinguish competing explanations are both accepted. Hypothetical records are supplied examples, not measurements by this app or universal physical laws.
- Stories: understand stated needs; explore possible responses; ask and revise after a reply. Twelve situations include noise/quiet, an agreed turn, unwanted touch, an accident, confusing rules, watching before joining, an unheard greeting, unsafe pushing, shared space, a startled child, a pause and a revised rest request. The diagrams associate participants with their stated information. Branches allow reasonable alternatives; later branches add checking, consent, tradeoffs and revision rather than larger quantities. Feelings and hidden intentions are not graded.

### Evidence and process support

Numbered reading details stay visible. Evidence alternatives quote actual fragments with their original line numbers, rather than presenting the entire passage against absurd unrelated guesses. Sufficient alternative bundles are accepted; a bundle may include additional information without becoming logically insufficient. These are authored interpretations and need ongoing editorial review, not an automated natural-language proof.

Subject panels use semantic table captions and headers: passages/clues, conditions, measurement records and participant requests. New grid pictures use 32px cells with separate larger choice containers. Visible rule keys and optional hints remain separate from the essential task. Earlier clues and supplied observations remain available during later phases; a neutral science prediction is not scored correct before observation.

Long text alternatives use the same full-width layout within a question. Single-letter spelling tiles retain their compact word-building layout. Menu previews are separately authored passive examples; they do not squeeze whole passages or record tables into small tiles or generate tasks. Necessary written content remains visible and may require adult narration.

### Authored scope and remaining breadth

The app now offers a broader range of reasoning within these five subjects, but not complete courses. Future distinct strands include Chinese character structure and oral retelling; separately verified English listening/phonological instruction; science habitats, weather/earth observations and engineering model tests; and richer narrative retelling, perspective comparison and practicing spoken communication. Add such strands through meaningful new learning goals and appropriate representations, not by renaming current tasks or increasing step counts alone. Do not infer readiness from a level number.

Curriculum references inform this original design: [IES reading comprehension guidance](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/14/i) emphasizes strategies, text structure, purposeful texts and discussion; [NGSS Appendix F](https://www.nextgenscience.org/sites/default/files/Appendix%20F%20%20Science%20and%20Engineering%20Practices%20in%20the%20NGSS%20-%20FINAL%20060513.pdf) describes investigation, analysis and evidence practices; [CASEL's framework](https://casel.org/fundamentals-of-sel/what-is-the-casel-framework/) includes relationships, social awareness and decision-making. These frameworks are not evidence that this app produces their outcomes or that these task levels map to their grade bands.

## Interaction and content contracts

- Every family contains reviewed real tasks. Practice continues manually at the same level with no fixed cap. Every family has at least 12 distinct content seeds. Banks shuffle; content signatures exclude IDs, feedback and choice positions. The previous ten content signatures per family are excluded and saved under `kll-<subject>-recent-v1` when storage works, including across reloads. The immediately preceding seed is also excluded within a loaded session. Science change/trial records vary bounded numeric data in frontend JavaScript (61 variants per record seed). Static narrative banks still eventually repeat; no endless unique-story claim is made. A future exhausted/undersized bank degrades safely instead of looping.
- Choice and final required selection/ordering complete immediately. Pictures/clues and completed objects are passive. Task content stays stable through wrong attempts, Hint and Undo. The visible cue identifies each phase; completion clears obsolete hints and Undo controls. Multi-stage questions retain the preceding story/prompt and reference pictures as passive clues, so evidence questions do not require recalling a vanished passage.
- Ordering validates all authored constraints, accepting every legal arrangement rather than a single witness. Language sequences with repeated identical tokens accept interchangeable tokens. Incorrect complete orders remain undoable.
- Science prediction is neutral and separate from the provided observation. Observe is an explicitly labeled stage transition, not a correctness submission. Datasets are authored observations for reasoning, not measurements made by this app or universal claims about materials.
- Social story branches explore possible consequences and needs. Feelings are not scored right or wrong. All paths terminate; physical boundaries and trusted-adult help remain valid options. A completion check means the path finished.
- Adults may narrate early language tasks and written logic/science/story clues. Do not claim independent three-year-old reading or infer ability from played marks. Chinese uses simplified live text; desktop rendering and actual Kindle rendering are separate checks.
- English currently covers vocabulary, print, spelling and comprehension. Silent letter sequencing is not phonics. Listening/phonemic tasks require a separately verified audio design; they are not delivered here.
- Academic frameworks in the expansion plan inform these original introductory strands. This is not a complete licensed curriculum, an evaluated intervention or evidence of learning gains.

These sources maintain original tasks and write `learning/content.json`. `learning/generator.js`, `learning/engine.js`, `learning/pictures.js`, `learning/subject-pictures.js` and `learning/style.css` are shared authoring sources. `tools/build_learning.py` embeds their content and monochrome PNG artwork into five self-contained HTML pages. It reuses a small existing math artwork pack, draws instructional diagrams with Pillow, and rasterizes 15 reviewed OpenMoji black SVGs with Inkscape. Original release-pinned SVGs, file mapping and CC BY-SA 4.0 license are retained in `assets/openmoji/`; attribution is available in each grown-up guide and `art-credits.html`. Object/location composites reuse the reviewed pictures. Evaluate noun recognition at the displayed monochrome size and preserve any depicted feature used by a clue; a shoe-sole clue must not depend on laces absent from the artwork. Run authoring then building after edits; commit sources and generated pages together. No runtime framework, task-time network or continuous animation is required. JavaScript parses as ES5; actual device CSS/API support remains unverified.

Storage uses separate `kll-<subject>-played-v1` keys, catches errors and never clears other origin storage. Marks indicate played families only, not mastery or saved question state. No account, synchronization, offline reopening or cross-device resume is promised.

## Durable repeat-quality rule

An uncapped session with two samples is not adequate varied practice: it repeats on the third turn. Gate every new family on at least twelve substantive seeds plus a recent-ten content test. Do not count renamed characters, reordered choices, changed IDs or more test iterations as meaningful new content. Parameterized records must keep answers, observations and explanation consistent. Story and language banks require editorial review; factual observations remain authored models. Persist only compact recent fingerprints, not private child answers or mastery scores. Storage failure preserves playable in-memory exclusions.
